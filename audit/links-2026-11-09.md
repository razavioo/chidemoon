# Production link audit — 2026-11-09

## Scope and result

- 1,868 distinct internal URLs fetched, including published products/content,
  categories, navigation, pagination, term archives and discovered seller redirects.
- 10,453 distinct rendered link targets after repairs; zero unresolved internal
  destinations, placeholder links, broken fragment targets or redirect loops.
- 5,132 linked media files checked with HTTP HEAD: all returned 200.
- 718 published-product merchant sources checked: 716 Basalam pages validated
  against embedded product identity and availability; two Digikala products checked
  in the rendered original product pages (EN-101 and C801), both available.
- All 684 seller redirect URLs discovered in rendered HTML matched their configured
  merchant product destination before availability changes, except blocked offers.
- No social-network links were present in the rendered pages. No social URL was
  invented or substituted.

## Repairs

Nine source products were no longer saleable: 7659, 7624, 5631, 3095, 3087, 2707,
2517, 939, 791. Recorded observed unavailability and hid them from the catalog;
their existing detail pages remain HTTP 200 with a Persian unavailable notice and
no seller CTA. Original observations/visibility and merchant evidence are retained
in `_chidemoon_before_link_audit_20261109` product metadata.

Saved /go/ links for publicly approved but unavailable products now redirect to
their own detail page, instead of an error or unavailable seller. Unpublished,
unreviewed and invalid-source products retain the rejection gate. Active offers
still redirect to their exact merchant product. Scene hotspots/home selection now
exclude unavailable offers.

Two accessibility skip links targeted a missing #chidemoon-content: the product
chooser (597) now has a main landmark with that ID; native Look pages use the
theme's existing #content landmark. Original chooser data is backed up in
`_chidemoon_before_skip_target_20261109`.

## Verification and evidence

Production PHP lint and existing affiliate-cta checks passed. Verified all nine
unavailable product pages, their saved redirects, and two active Digikala redirects.
Browser checks covered logo/navigation/footer, a live comparison with products
443/447, seller links and both repaired skip-link targets. Audit requests used DNT
for tracking endpoints to avoid adding synthetic clicks.

The repaired URLs were fetched again and merged into the crawl evidence: final
analysis reports zero broken internal links and zero broken anchors (1,888 requests
including 20 repair checks, 1,868 distinct URLs).
